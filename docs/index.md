# eCommerce store budget in India: the four invoices to total before you compare quotes

*By Tarun Kumar · 15 November 2026*

A developer's quote covers one line item: the build. Three more arrive on other invoices — the platform subscription, a fee on every order, and upkeep such as domain, hosting and email. Total all four before comparing any two quotes; the cheapest build can still be the most expensive store to run.

## The four lines

| Line item | Billed | Number comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Build (design, development, setup) | One-time | Developer's quote |
| Platform subscription | Monthly | Shopify's pricing page, or your host for WooCommerce |
| Payment gateway | Per successful order | Gateway's pricing page |
| Upkeep (domain, hosting, email, maintenance) | Monthly / yearly | Registrar, host, Google Workspace, retainer |

A quote covering only the first row is a quarter of the story.

## Line 1: the platform

Shopify lists four India plans, each charging a fee on orders paid through third-party payment providers. Confirm live prices at [shopify.com/in/pricing](https://www.shopify.com/in/pricing).

| Plan | Monthly, billed yearly | Monthly, billed monthly | Third-party provider fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | ₹1,499 | ₹1,994 | 2% |
| Grow | ₹5,599 | ₹7,447 | 1% |
| Advanced | ₹22,680 | ₹30,164 | 0.6% |
| Plus | From ₹1,75,000 | — | 0.2% |

[WooCommerce](https://woocommerce.com/) is open source and a free download — no monthly platform fee — but you still pay for hosting and any paid extensions or themes. It moves the platform line into hosting and add-ons rather than removing it.

## Line 2: the gateway

Razorpay's standard rate is 2% per successful domestic transaction across cards, UPI and netbanking, with 18% GST charged on the fee (not the order value), ₹0 setup and ₹0 annual maintenance. A ₹1,000 order costs ₹20 plus ₹3.60 GST — ₹23.60 in all. Gigzman's pricing page lists the gateway at roughly 2% + GST. Verify current rates at [razorpay.com/pricing](https://razorpay.com/pricing/).

## The math on one ₹1-lakh month

Shopify Basic (yearly billing) with Razorpay as a third-party provider, ₹1,00,000 in orders:

- Subscription: ₹1,499
- Shopify third-party fee (2%): ₹2,000
- Razorpay fee (2%): ₹2,000
- GST at 18% on the Razorpay fee: ₹360
- **Total: ₹5,859** — before domain, apps, email and shipping

The same month on Grow: ₹5,599 + ₹1,000 (1% fee) + ₹2,000 + ₹360 = ₹8,959. The plans differ by ₹4,100 a month and one percentage point of fees, so Grow only pays for itself above ₹4,10,000 of monthly sales through a third-party gateway (₹4,100 ÷ 0.01). Below that, pick Grow for its features, not to save on fees.

## Line 3: the build

Four factors decide most of a build quote:

- The number of distinct page layouts and screens
- Whether a design exists — designing from scratch adds two to four weeks
- Integrations (payments, CRM, ERP), each its own project; undocumented systems cost more
- Who writes the content and product copy

eCommerce scope adds more: variants, filters and search that work on mobile; B2B pricing such as quantity breaks, customer-group pricing and quote requests; inventory and order statuses with notification emails; GA4 ecommerce events; and migration of products, variants, customers and orders.

## Line 4: upkeep — and what quotes leave out

- **Product photography** — Gigzman, for one, does not shoot it
- **Content writing**
- **A URL redirect map** on migration — the part that protects your search traffic
- **Running costs**: domain ₹800–₹1,500 a year; hosting ₹0–₹2,000 a month for a typical marketing site (a WooCommerce or custom store may need more); Google Workspace email ₹150–₹900 per user per month; an optional maintenance retainer of ₹8,000–₹35,000 a month

As the [pricing page](https://gigzman.com/pricing?utm_source=github-pages&utm_medium=guest&utm_campaign=gp) puts it, "a build quote that ignores them is a quote that surprises you in month two".

## When to wait

If you are still testing whether the product sells, do not commission a store — sell through a marketplace or an Instagram link first. The four lines above only make sense against revenue that already exists.
